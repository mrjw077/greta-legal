Contact us

Write to support@greta.press and we will reply within 2 business days. Please tell us your device model, your iOS or Android version, and what happened — it helps us answer faster.

Frequent questions

I bought a subscription but Premium is not active

Open Profile and use Restore purchases. Premium is tied to your account, so it comes back after reinstalling the app or switching devices. If that does not help, write to us and include the e-mail you signed in with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store, not by us. On iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play → Payments and subscriptions. You can also open it from Profile in the app.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → App settings → Delete account. This removes your data from our servers permanently. You can also request deletion here.

Is Greta a medical service?

No. Greta does not diagnose and does not replace a doctor. Predictions are estimates based on the data you enter. For any health question please see a specialist.
